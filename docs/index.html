How it works

  1. Hold your key — Caps Lock unless you pick another one.
  2. Speak. A small overlay shows that hushkey is listening.
  3. Let go. The transcript is pasted into the focused window.

The speech model runs on your own machine. After the one-time model download (about 0.5–1.5 GB on first use), hushkey needs no network at all — nothing you say is sent anywhere. A GPU is used when there is one, otherwise the CPU does the work.

What you need

WindowsWindows 10 or newer, 64-bit.
LinuxA desktop session (X11 or Wayland), glibc 2.35 or newer — Ubuntu 22.04 and later, Debian 12 and later. Dictating reads the key from /dev/input, so the installer puts you in the input group; that takes one logout to apply.
macOSmacOS 13 or newer. Allow hushkey under Accessibility when asked — that is what lets it see the key and type for you.

Questions

Does anything I say leave my computer?

No. Recognition happens locally. The only downloads are the speech model on first use and update checks against GitHub, which you can switch off.

Which languages?

Everything whisper handles — over 100 languages. German and English are what hushkey is tested with most.

Can I change the key?

Yes, in the tray menu. Caps Lock is the default because few people miss it; Right Alt, Right Ctrl and Scroll Lock are offered too.

How do I remove it?

Windows: through the app list. Linux: sudo apt remove hushkey, or hushkey --uninstall for a plain-binary install. macOS: hushkey --uninstall. Your settings and the downloaded model stay unless you delete them yourself.
